## Notes on Methodology

- All positions sized at $1,000 USD equivalent at entry (using previous close price)
- European positions tracked in native currency; P&L converted to USD for summary using day's EUR/USD or GBP/USD close
- Stop-loss: −15% | T1 take-profit: +25% (sell 1/3) | T2 take-profit: +50% (sell 1/3) | Signal exit: TA + Burry overlay (no time limit)
- Entry price = previous close on date the trade is recorded
- No slippage, commission, or spread modelled (pure simulation)
- Strategy rotation: 7 strategies (A–G); no strategy repeated on consecutive days, softly weighted by the weekly performance review loop (below)
- **Capital cap: €30,000 EUR max base invested** (added 2026-08-03, decreased from €40,000 2026-08-03) — sum of remaining cost basis across currently open positions only, excl. all realised/unrealised P&L, converted at the day's EUR/USD close
- **Active management (added 2026-08-03):** daily discretionary authority to exit, trim, or add to positions beyond the mechanical stop/T1/T2/signal rules, to optimize performance or free capacity under the cap — any discretionary cash event (exit/trim/add) is logged as its own Equity Curve row like a mechanical one
- **Performance review loop (added 2026-08-03):** weekly (first trading day), review this page's Strategy Breakdown table and lean the coming week's strategy selection toward stronger realised win rate/net P&L and away from weaker ones (never a hard exclusion)
- **Portfolio-size discipline (observed 2026-08-28):** when a new trade would push the open-position count above 35, the weakest position by P&L%/thesis staleness may be exited under Step 3F discretionary authority even without a mechanical or signal trigger, logged as a 🔵 discretionary exit
