## Notes on Methodology

- All positions sized at $1,000 USD equivalent at entry (using previous close price)
- European positions tracked in native currency; P&L converted to USD for summary using day's EUR/USD or GBP/USD close
- Stop-loss: −15% | T1 take-profit: +25% (sell 1/3) | T2 take-profit: +50% (sell 1/3) | Signal exit: TA + Burry overlay (no time limit)
- Entry price = previous close on date the trade is recorded
- No slippage, commission, or spread modelled (pure simulation)
- Strategy rotation: 7 strategies (A–G); no strategy repeated on consecutive days
